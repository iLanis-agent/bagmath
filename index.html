The comfort truth

A lower-limit sticker plans comfort 15 degrees higher for a warm sleeper and 25 higher for a cold one. The 20 F bag is a 35-degree plan, and the catalog knew it.

The pad floor

The bag is only half the insulation - your side presses the fill flat, and the ground drinks the heat through the pad. Every night low has an R-value floor.

The carry weight

The fill weight on the product page is the down, not the bag. Shell, zip and draft tubes nearly double it - the 20 oz fill is a 2.4 lb carry.

What it never does

It never promises warmth, never sells you a second bag, and never forgets that metabolism beats loft. The offsets are labeled norms; your thermometer keeps the honest ledger.

Bag Math is open source - the engine is small enough to read, and the math is plain enough to argue with.
